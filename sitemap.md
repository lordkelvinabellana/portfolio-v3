# Sitemap for Developer Portfolio

## Recommended Page Hierarchy

1. **Home**
   - Introduction and strong first impression
2. **About**
   - Brief overview of the developer
3. **Experience**
   - Work experience with a focus on relevant projects and achievements
4. **Projects**
   - Showcase of completed projects with detailed case studies
5. **Skills**
   - List of technical skills and tools
6. **Credentials**
   - Certifications, badges, and other credentials
7. **Contact**
   - Form or method for recruiters to get in touch  


## Section Order

1. **Home** (First impression)
2. **About** (Background and qualifications)
3. **Experience** (Work history and achievements)
4. **Projects** (Portfolio of completed projects)
5. **Skills** (Technical expertise)
6. **Credentials** (Verification of skills and experience)
7. **Contact** (Recruiter engagement)  


## Navigation Flow

1. Home
2. About
3. Experience
4. Projects
5. Skills
6. Credentials
7. Contact  


## Reasoning Behind Each Decision

- **Home**: Start with a strong first impression to grab the recruiter's attention.
- **About**: Provide context and background, establishing credibility.
- **Experience**: Highlight relevant work experience and achievements.
- **Projects**: Showcase completed projects with detailed case studies.
- **Skills**: List technical skills and tools for recruiters to understand capabilities.
- **Credentials**: Verify skills and experience through certifications and badges.
- **Contact**: Provide a method for recruiters to get in touch.  


## Recruiter-First UX Thinking

- **Intuitive Flow**: Ensure the site is easy to navigate and understand from a recruiter's perspective.
- **Clear Messaging**: Use clear and concise language throughout the portfolio.
- **Trust Signals**: Place trust signals like certifications, badges, and project case studies prominently.
- **Credibility Placement**: Present credentials and achievements in a way that builds trust.  


## Business Value Presentation

- **Highlight Achievements**: Emphasize key achievements and results from past projects.
- **Showcase Impact**: Demonstrate the impact of completed projects on clients or businesses.
- **Value Proposition**: Clearly communicate the value the developer brings to potential employers.  


## Trust Signals and Credibility Placement

- **Certifications and Badges**: Place certifications and badges prominently in the About and Credentials sections.
- **Project Case Studies**: Include detailed case studies with results and impact metrics.
- **Experience Timeline**: Present work experience in a chronological order, highlighting relevant projects.
